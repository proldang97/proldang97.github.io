PR7 Burger

A three-meat burger with cheese, bacon, onion rings and air-fryer fries.

Watch the video (TikTok)

Ingredients Tick what you already have.

Method

  1. Cut the potatoes and boil them for 5 minutes.
  2. Drain and dry them, toss with olive oil and air-fry at 180 °C (350 °F) for 20 minutes.
  3. For the onion rings: cut the onion into large, thick rings.
  4. Bread them in this order: flour, egg, then panko.
  5. Fry the breaded rings until golden brown.
  6. Mix the three ground meats and season with Worcestershire sauce, salt and garlic powder.
  7. Shape the patties and cook them on a griddle.
  8. A few minutes before they are done, put the cheese on top so it melts.
  9. Fry the bacon in a pan until golden brown.
  10. Spread the sauces on the bun to taste.
  11. Build your burger.
  12. Enjoy.
