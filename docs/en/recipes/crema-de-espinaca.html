Cream of spinach soup

A smooth soup of spinach, broccoli and potato, finished with a swirl of cream and crunchy potato chips.

Watch the video (TikTok)

Ingredients Tick what you already have.

Method

  1. Peel and dice the potatoes. Chop the onion and cut the broccoli into florets.
  2. Put the potato, onion and broccoli in a pot and add the spinach on top.
  3. Cover with water, put the lid on and boil until the potato is tender (about 15–20 min).
  4. Transfer the vegetables to a blender with some of the cooking water and blend until smooth. Add more liquid if you want it thinner.
  5. Pour the soup back into the pot, add a cube of butter, a few drops of Worcestershire sauce, and salt and pepper to taste. Stir and leave it covered on low heat for a few minutes.
  6. Serve in a deep bowl and garnish with a swirl of cream and crushed potato chips.
