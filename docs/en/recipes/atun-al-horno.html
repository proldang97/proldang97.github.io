Baked tuna

Layers of mashed potato, tuna and a tomato-pepper sauté, with melted cheese and a golden gratin on top.

Watch the video (TikTok)

Ingredients Tick what you already have.

Method

  1. Cook the potatoes and mash them.
  2. Sauté the garlic, pepper and tomato in olive oil.
  3. Grease a baking dish with olive oil and add a layer of mashed potato.
  4. Add the tuna and the sauté on top.
  5. Cover with sliced cheese and another layer of mashed potato.
  6. Finish with grated cheese and herbs, and bake until golden.
