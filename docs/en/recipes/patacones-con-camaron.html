Patacones with shrimp

Crispy fried plantain topped with shrimp in pink sauce, red onion and cilantro.

Watch the video (TikTok)

Ingredients Tick what you already have.

Method

  1. Clean the shrimp.
  2. Marinate the shrimp in lime juice and let them rest.
  3. Cut the green plantain in half.
  4. Fry the plantain in hot oil for about two minutes.
  5. Take the plantain out and flatten it while it is still soft.
  6. Put the flattened plantain back in the oil and finish frying.
  7. Chop the red onion and cilantro very finely.
  8. Make the sauce: half ketchup and half mayonnaise with black pepper, until you get a pink sauce.
  9. Mix the shrimp with the sauce, onion and cilantro.
  10. Serve the shrimp on top of the patacones.
  11. Enjoy.
