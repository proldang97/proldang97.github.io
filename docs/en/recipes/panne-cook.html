Panne Cook

A round bread bowl filled with bacon, beef and mushrooms in a creamy white sauce.

Watch the video (TikTok)

Ingredients Tick what you already have.

Method

  1. Cut the bacon into thin strips and fry.
  2. Cut the beef into thin strips and fry. Season with salt and garlic powder.
  3. Fry the mushrooms.
  4. For the sauce: toast the flour in a pan, stirring, until it turns light golden.
  5. Mix in the butter until you get a thick paste.
  6. Pour in the milk and stir well until there are no lumps.
  7. When it starts to thicken, season with the cream, black pepper, a few drops of Worcestershire sauce and salt to taste.
  8. Add the bacon, beef and mushrooms to the sauce.
  9. Cut open the top of the bread and pull out the soft inside.
  10. Pour everything into the bread.
  11. Enjoy.
