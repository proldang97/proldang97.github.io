About me

Hi, I'm Pablo Roldán Gómez, an adventurous paisa who can't sit still. 🌍

A few years ago I started my recipe channel as a hobby. I've always loved cooking, and one day a friend suggested I post my food on social media because it looked really good.

I started on TikTok while I was still living in Medellín, and to my surprise my recipes began attracting a big audience. My videos got 10 to 20 times more views than the followers I had, and that motivated me to keep going. Today my recipes are also on YouTube and Instagram. Follow me on all of them:

TikTokYouTubeInstagram

Two years later I moved to Valencia, Spain, to do a master's in Digital Marketing, because social media and online business always caught my attention. But living with flatmates, studying, working and having a social life made me put aside this project, even though it had been going really well. 🥲

It's never too late to take the reins again and refocus. Now, in Madrid, I'm sure this project has a lot of potential, and with your help we're going to make it happen. 🔥

Follow me on social media and check out all my recipes. Leave me comments and tell me what you'd like to see. Little by little I'll shape this website into an experience that lives up to the channel. 🤙🏼

As we'd say in Colombia… se vienen cositas (big things are coming) 😏🔝
